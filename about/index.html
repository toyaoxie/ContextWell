About

Building Human Capacity in Context

ContextWell Lab is an independent research and innovation lab exploring how human capacity develops within the contexts in which people learn, work, receive care, and participate in society.

We study how these contexts shape what people are able to understand, decide, do, and become — and how they can be designed to better support human capacity and well-being.

Our Guiding Principle

Well-being should not be added after a solution is designed. It should be embedded in how solutions are conceived, developed, evaluated, and evolved.

Well-being, embedded by design.

Operating Logic

Five questions guide every project

  1. 1. What is the context?
  2. 2. What human capacity matters here?
  3. 3. What conditions shape it?
  4. 4. What can we change?
  5. 5. How is well-being embedded in the solution?
