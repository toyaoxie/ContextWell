About

Perspective

How we think about change: a perspective that connects context, human capacity, and well-being.

Our Guiding Principle

Well-being should not be added after a solution is designed. It should inform how problems are understood, how solutions are developed, and how change is evaluated.

Well-being, embedded by design.

Our Perspective

Context → Human Capacity → Change

Context

Human development takes place within contexts shaped by environments, systems, relationships, technologies, and wider conditions.

Human Capacity

These contexts can influence what people are able to learn, adapt, decide, act, relate, and develop.

Change

Research and innovation can help create better conditions for human development and well-being.

ContextWell Lab explores these relationships across different domains, translating knowledge into possibilities for change.

From Research to Change

From understanding to action

Understanding

We study how contexts influence human experience, capacity, and well-being.

Building

We translate knowledge into research, frameworks, interventions, tools, and other forms of practical innovation.

Evolving

We remain attentive to changing contexts, emerging needs, and new knowledge as solutions develop.

Our perspective provides a common direction across our work, while the methods, frameworks, and approaches we use are developed according to the needs of each project and context.
