Research Programme

Our mission is to foster a well-being-driven world. The Research Programme is how we work to get there: three methods, one programme.

Research

Developing frameworks — starting with SCWF — that make contextual well-being and human capacity legible, so they can be studied and designed for rather than assumed.

Experimentation

Building applied pilots, like Well-being Builder, that let people practise judgement and well-being as a skill under realistic, contextual conditions.

Translation

Turning findings into research services, reports, and practice recommendations for partner organisations across education, healthcare, and governance.

Foundation

Where this starts: SCWF

Our doctoral research, the Spectrum of Contextual Well-being Framework (SCWF), is the foundation of this programme. It answers the first layer of our operating logic — how to identify and understand the conditions within a context that shape well-being.

Future research extends outward from this foundation, toward capacity building, intervention, solution design, and system-level change — across education, healthcare, organisations, and AI-mediated environments.
